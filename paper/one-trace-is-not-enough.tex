\begin{abstract}
Calibration and safety claims for agent-authored controllers are routinely demonstrated with a single execution.
We argue that such claims are \emph{2-safety hyperproperties}: the bad thing is not one run, but a \emph{coupled pair} that shares all exogenous inputs except one controlled perturbation.
\textbf{DIPTYCH} is an IEEE-oriented artifact harness that forks a shared prefix (open-loop shape or common-random-numbers closed-loop point), discards incomparable pairs, and grades suffixes only.
It ships schema-\texttt{0.2} probe envelopes, eight operators spanning open-loop and CRN closed-loop regimes, and a \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate} power-on-axis check that rejects cosmetic verdict flips.
Product adapters (ZeroDay, AOMB) emit conforming/violating twins at pinned SHAs; DIPTYCH grades and does not vendor those trees.
This paper specifies the harness, operator inventory, adapter case placements, and an offline evaluation \emph{protocol}.
We invent no AUROC or model scores---only harness behavior, pin facts, and coverage-cell criteria.
\end{abstract}

hyperproperties, 2-safety, agent-authored controllers, paired-trace grading, CRN coupling, software artifacts

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Agent-authored control stacks---LLM-assisted planners, security triage agents, ranking and closed-loop simulators---are often shipped with a \emph{single-trace demo}: one seed, one schedule, one cassette replay.
A lone film can look legal while hiding that the claimed calibration depends on an unstated coupling (shared clock, shared noise, shared schema, shared polarity convention).
Single-run demos therefore overclaim.

\paragraph{Hyperproperty framing.}
Clarkson and Schneider's hyperproperties lift properties from individual traces to sets of traces~\cite{clarkson2010hyperproperties}.
Calibration-as-robustness under a controlled axis is a classic \emph{2-safety} pattern: for every comparable pair related by a declared perturbation, a relational verdict must hold.
DIPTYCH operationalizes that pattern for agent-authored controllers.

\paragraph{Film metaphor.}
One reel may be admissible under local invariants.
Calibration is the \emph{diptych}: two frames that share a prefix and differ on exactly one graded axis.
Incomparable pairs are discarded, not papered over with a score.
\emph{One film $=$ legal; only the pair $=$ calibrated.}

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{DIPTYCH harness} --- schema~\texttt{0.2} envelopes, eight operators, gates including \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate}.
  \item \textbf{Operator inventory} --- FREEZEDRY, RESEED, SCHEMAX, SIGNFLIP, SATEXTEND, HISTSWAP, TRAJSWAP, VARSCALE.
  \item \textbf{Adapter contract} --- ZeroDay and AOMB emit twins; DIPTYCH grades; product trees are pinned, not forked into this repository.
  \item \textbf{Coverage matrix} --- Operator $\times$ \{\texttt{diptych\_core}, \texttt{zeroday}, \texttt{aomb}\}; green iff twin contrast \emph{and} axis power.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Non-claims.}
Localization $\neq$ exploitability.
No exploit/PoC payloads.
No AUROC / model-score fields in graded envelopes.
Adapter case placements cite pinned SHAs only---never invented model scores.

\section{Related Work and Hyperproperty Taxonomy}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Hyperproperties and $k$-safety}
Hyperproperties classify requirements over \emph{sets} of executions~\cite{clarkson2010hyperproperties}.
\emph{2-safety} hyperproperties are falsified by a finite pair of traces.
Relational verification, product programs, and hyperproperty runtime verification study the same shape: couple two runs, then check a relational assertion~\cite{barthe2011relational,finkbeiner2019rv}.
DIPTYCH does not claim a new hyperproperty calculus; it supplies an \emph{executable grading harness} and operator taxonomy tuned to agent-authored control artifacts.

Common Random Numbers (CRN) couple stochastic closed-loop rollouts so residuals and variance scales are attributable to the declared axis rather than to independent noise draws.

Verdict vocabulary is exactly $\{\texttt{pass}, \texttt{fail}, \texttt{inconclusive}\}$.
\texttt{inconclusive} requires a documented reason and does \emph{not} paint a coverage cell green.

\section{DIPTYCH Model}
\label{sec:model}

\subsection{Shared prefix, suffix probes}
DIPTYCH forks a shared prefix---open-loop shape or CRN closed-loop point---then probes \emph{suffixes} under one operator.
Pairs that violate the shared-prefix / CRN identity rule are incomparable and are not scored as green.

\subsection{Schema \texttt{0.2} envelope}
Hard keys (contract): \texttt{diptych\_schema}, \texttt{source}, \texttt{operator}, \texttt{coupling}, \texttt{probe\_id}, \texttt{control\_role}, \texttt{traces} (length $\geq 2$), \texttt{expected\_verdict}.
Sources: $\{\texttt{diptych\_core}, \texttt{zeroday}, \texttt{aomb}\}$.
TRAJSWAP and VARSCALE \emph{must} use \texttt{coupling: crn\_closed\_loop}.
Forbidden fields include \texttt{auroc}, \texttt{AUROC}, \texttt{lab\_auroc}, and \texttt{model\_grade} (contract rejection).

\subsection{Artifact layout}
Core package under \texttt{diptych/} (contract, grade, gates, \texttt{mutate\_axis}, CRN helpers);
per-operator \texttt{ops/<op>/} and handwritten \texttt{controls/<op>/};
fixture twins under \texttt{diptych-probes/};
live cells in \texttt{coverage/matrix.json};
read-only pins in \texttt{adapters/PINS.md}.

A coverage cell is \textbf{green} only when (1)~conforming~$\to$~\texttt{pass}, (2)~violating~$\to$~\texttt{fail}, and (3)~\texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate} reports \texttt{power\_ok}.

\subsection{Offline PoC command}
No GPU and no network are required for the core PoC.
From the repository root:
\begin{verbatim}
./scripts/run_poc.sh
# equivalent: make poc
\end{verbatim}
The script runs \texttt{python3 -m diptych.run\_full8} (manifest $\to$ stubs $\to$ contrast $\to$ axis $\to$ \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate} $\to$ write \texttt{coverage/matrix.json}) then unit tests.
Exit code~$0$ means all eight \texttt{diptych\_core} cells are green under the protocol of Section~\ref{sec:eval}.

\subsection{What we refuse to report}
AUROC, lab AUROC, or invented model grades as hyperproperty scores;
exploit / attack / PoC payload fields;
green adapter cells without pin evidence.

\section{Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:eval}

This section specifies the evaluation \emph{protocol}.
It does \textbf{not} invent AUROC, accuracy, ranking lifts, or other model scores.

\subsection{Protocol object}
Evaluate Operator $\times$ \{\texttt{diptych\_core}, \texttt{zeroday}, \texttt{aomb}\} cells written to \texttt{coverage/matrix.json}.
Each cell records a categorical status (\texttt{green} / \texttt{pending} / \texttt{stub} / \ldots) and a boolean \texttt{axis\_power}.
No numeric model metric is part of the grade.

\subsection{Steps (offline, deterministic)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Checkout this repository; set \texttt{PYTHONPATH=.}.
  \item Run \texttt{./scripts/run\_poc.sh} (or \texttt{make poc}).
  \item Confirm exit code~$0$.
  \item Confirm \texttt{coverage/matrix.json} lists eight operators with \texttt{diptych\_core=green} and \texttt{axis\_power=true}.
  \item For adapter columns: attribute \texttt{zeroday}/\texttt{aomb} status only via pin facts in Table~\ref{tab:pins} and adapter QC evidence---do not invent scores; do not vendor product trees into this repo.
  \item Negative controls (unit tests): cosmetic mutators (expected-verdict-only flips, SARIF level renames, AUROC injection) must \emph{not} change the axis fingerprint and therefore must fail \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Green rule (repeatable criterion)}
A cell is green iff all hold:
\begin{itemize}
  \item conforming control $\to$ grader \texttt{pass};
  \item violating control $\to$ grader \texttt{fail};
  \item \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate} power-on-axis succeeds on the conforming twin;
  \item no stub markers; traces length $\geq 2$; CRN ops tagged \texttt{crn\_closed\_loop}.
\end{itemize}
\texttt{inconclusive} $\neq$ green.

\subsection{Citation policy for metrics}
Cite only: repository facts, CI exit codes, and cells written to \texttt{coverage/matrix.json}.
Do not invent AUROC, accuracy, or model-score numbers for this artifact.
Live matrix notes in-repo state that adapter greens (when recorded) cite ZeroDay@\texttt{fb5b39da} and AOMB@\texttt{667e475} with \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate}---not fabricated model studies.

\subsection{Reproducibility command}
\begin{verbatim}
./scripts/run_poc.sh   # expect exit 0; refreshes coverage/matrix.json
\end{verbatim}

\section{Limitations, Ethics, and Non-Claims}
\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Internal.}
Fixture twins under \texttt{diptych\_core} are handwritten controls; they establish protocol power, not statistical generalization across production fleets.
Graders are deterministic functions of envelope fields---bugs in graders would systematically mislabel cells.

\paragraph{Construct.}
``Green'' means twin contrast + axis power under schema~\texttt{0.2}, not exploitability, not deployment safety certification, and not model quality.

\paragraph{External.}
Adapter columns are pinned case placements (Table~\ref{tab:pins}); product trees are not re-derived inside this repository.
A pin move without a matching QC would drop those columns out of green.

\paragraph{Ethics.}
Desk-safe fingerprints and fixtures; omit exploit/payload fields from artifacts.
Localization $\neq$ exploitability.
No AUROC / model scores in graded envelopes.
\texttt{inconclusive} $\neq$ green.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:concl}

DIPTYCH treats calibration of agent-authored controllers as a \emph{2-safety} grading problem over coupled traces.
The harness couples prefixes, applies one of eight operators, requires asymmetric twin verdicts, and demands \texttt{gate\_axis\_mutate} power-on-axis.
ZeroDay@\texttt{fb5b39da} and AOMB@\texttt{667e475} are adapter case placements that emit twins; DIPTYCH grades.
The evaluation protocol in Section~\ref{sec:eval} records coverage cells and axis power---never invented model scores.
One film can be legal; only the pair is calibrated.
